Docusaurus resolves frontmatter 'slug' relative to routeBasePath, so the frozen canonical slug '/concepts/<name>' produced '/concepts/concepts/<name>' under routeBasePath 'concepts'. Mounting at the site root reproduces the frozen slugs exactly while src/pages still owns /, /search, /explore, /ask and /about (verified: each route renders its own h1).

- Eleven confirmed technical errors found by adversarial review were corrected in the slice, each re-derived independently before the fix. (1-3) cross-correlation.md: under the page's own convention the reflection falls on the FIRST argument, so the identity is f*g = f-tilde conv g, not f conv g-tilde; the worked example was relabelled so the kernel occupies the template slot, its displacement ranges stated explicitly, and the symmetric-kernel case rebuilt with a kernel symmetric about the origin ([1,2,1] at -1..1) because [1,1] at 0..1 is symmetric about 0.5 and the two operations then agree only up to a shift. (4) convolutional-layer.md no longer calls the 1998 paper the original formulation of the trainable layer; the 1989 priority is stated and explicitly marked as unsupported by any source cited on the page. (5) pooling.md: local averaging with subsampling is the neocognitron's C-layer, not the S-layer. (6) backpropagation-through-convolution.md summary said 'convolution with the flipped kernel', which is the double flip the page itself warns against. (7-9) vgg.md: channels double at the first three pooling stages not four; activation volume falls by half only while channels double and by a quarter afterwards; compute is spread almost evenly per layer across blocks 1-4 with blocks 3 and 4 largest at 29.9% each, not concentrated in block 1, which is the second cheapest. (10-11) resnet.md: 3.57% top-5 is the winning six-model ensemble, not a single ResNet-152 (4.49%); and ResNet-152's lower arithmetic cost comes from its aggressive stem and bottleneck trunk, since VGG's dense head is under 1% of its multiply-accumulates.
- The 1989 paper 'Backpropagation Applied to Handwritten Zip Code Recognition' was NOT added as a source: the MIT Press page returns HTTP 403 to a bounded request, the IEEE mirror returns 202 without a body, and the only 200 response is an http-only PDF whose contents could not be verified here. Rather than cite an unverified URL, the claim is stated in prose and explicitly marked unsupported, as AGENT_CONTENT_CONTRACT.md requires.
- A ranking bug was found by the D04 tests and fixed: the final sort broke ties by title, which discarded BM25 ordering and put 'Pooling' above 'Translation Equivariance' for 'translation invariance'. Bands are now sorted by band only, with insertion order as the tie-break, because each band is already internally ordered.
